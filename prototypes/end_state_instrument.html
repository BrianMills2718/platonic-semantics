A structure that dissolves under scrutiny

Not a map. What this project is actually trying to measure is a refinement lattice — nested groupings that survive only as long as enough independent observers endorse them. Raise the bar and the fine distinctions go first; the coarse skeleton goes last.

A two-dimensional scatter cannot show this, because the object is not a space. It is a hierarchy with a survival threshold, and the interesting quantity is what breaks.

What this threshold costs

Why not a scatter plotA 2-D plot of word embeddings shows one system's geometry, and anyone can make one from any embedding in ten minutes. It cannot show a grouping that four observers accept and two reject — which is the only thing this project exists to measure.
Why the threshold is the controlEvery observer sees a coarsening of the structure. Rather than picking one and calling it the answer, the instrument makes the level explicit and names what each one discards.
Why heterogeneous observersLanguage models, a vision encoder, human judgement and a formal ontology are partial views of one thing. Putting a symbolic system in the roster lets a formal structure check a neural one, instead of only comparing neural to neural.
